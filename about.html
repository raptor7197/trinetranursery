Welcome to Trinetra

Creating Green Sanctuaries for Over Two Decades

Trinetra Nursery began with a simple vision: to bring the beauty and health benefits of nature into every home and workspace. What started as a small family passion has grown into one of the most respected nurseries in the East Godavari region.

We take immense pride in our scientific approach to plant cultivation, ensuring every sapling that leaves our nursery is healthy, resilient, and of the highest quality. Our team of expert horticulturists works tirelessly to maintain our vast collection of native and exotic species.

Our Vision

To provide premium plants and professional landscaping services that inspire and sustain a greener planet.
