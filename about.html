About Trinetra Nursery

Our Story

Founded on Quality, Grown with Passion

Trinetra Nursery is one of the premier destinations in the Kadiyam region, celebrated for its top-notch quality and service. Established with a vision to provide the best plant species and landscaping expertise, we have grown from a small plot to a multi-acre facility.

We specialize in a vast variety of plants, from indoor beauties to exotic outdoor collections. Our team of experienced horticulturists is dedicated to ensuring that every plant you receive is healthy, vibrant, and ready to thrive.
