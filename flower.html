Flower Plants

100+ Varieties

Trinetra Nursery offers one of the largest collections of flowering plants in Andhra Pradesh. From vibrant seasonal blooms to perennial favorites, our nursery is home to over 100 varieties of flower plants — including roses, marigolds, jasmine, hibiscus, adenium, bougainvillea, crossandra, and many more. Each plant is nurtured with organic care in our Kadiyam greenhouse, ensuring healthy roots, strong growth, and abundant flowering. Whether you're looking to brighten your home garden, create a commercial flower bed, or source wholesale quantities for landscaping projects, we have the right varieties for every season and purpose. Our team provides expert advice on plant selection, soil preparation, and bloom cycles so you get the best results. Visit our nursery to explore the blooms in person, or place an order for pan-India delivery — carefully packed and dispatched fresh.
