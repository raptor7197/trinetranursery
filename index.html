Welcome to Trinetra Nursery

Rooted in Quality Since 1998

Founded by P.V.V.S.N. Murthy, Trinetra Nursery has been a cornerstone of the Kadiyam plant community for over two decades. We specialize in providing high-quality indoor plants, fruit trees, and ornamental shrubs to garden enthusiasts and commercial landscapers alike.

Our mission is to bring nature closer to your life by offering the finest selection of plants and expert gardening advice. We take pride in our sustainable growing practices and our commitment to customer satisfaction.
